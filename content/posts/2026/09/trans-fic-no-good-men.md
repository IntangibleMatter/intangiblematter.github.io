***Please take all of this with a grain of salt. I'm just talking based on
things I've noticed in my own experience. I'm not trying to inadvertently argue
with a really good piece of transfeminist theory I haven't read. These are my
thoughts and observations based on my own experience.***

*Also spoilers for **Sisters of Dorley**, **Godfeels**, and some other things.*

So I've read a fair amount of the popular internet transfem fics over the last
few years, and while they're all fairly good (or at least they are for some
parts of them) I've noticed a trend: men are almost always either villains, or
somehow going to be made to not be a man later in the story. (Or a
non-character, that's an option too.)

Now, there's at least one fairly obvious reason for this: most of these stories
are written by lesbian trans women. Men are obviously not going to be the things
they're most interested in writing. Which, fair enough, but I do also think we
should discuss it anyways, because uncritically consuming your media is a bad
habit most of us are prone to a lot of the time. (I'm not exempt from this.)

One of the places that I first noticed this was in *Sisters of Dorley*, which
I'll assume you're at least vaguely familiar with given that you're even reading
this article in the first place. It's got a large ensemble cast, mostly composed
of trans women, which makes sense given the subject matter. In that ensemble
cast there are barely any men. You have:

- Several non-character men who barely have names (a few trans men included)
- Indira's boyfriend (non-character)
- Jake (one of the most despicable characters)
- Crispin Smyth-Farrow (villain from the past, only his influence is felt)
- Alistair Smyth-Farrow (Crispin's son, villain, but less of one than his sister)
- Trevor Darling (gay man, only man who's a character and not a villain)

Trevor Darling is exposed to grandmother's forced feminization process, but he's
a man who's too attached to being a man to be able to cope with it in the way
many other characters are. He's given large fake breasts, which (in the latest
chapter, spoiler warning) he gets taken out surgically before he's safely able
to do so because *he just wants the damn things off*. He's a sympathetic
character, he was working for the hall-allied PMC before getting pulled into
some deep shit. And he's the *only sympathetic man in the entire series that has
more than one or two lines of dialogue*. He's a gay man who's subjected to
involuntary transition (and then he detransitions, and is notably said to be
more like a trans man several times by different characters.) Every other man is
barely present, or just straight up a villain. If you have a counterexample,
here's my counterargument in advance: if they were a true counterexample, I
would've remembered them because they were an actual character.

The other place I noticed this initially was in the Homestuck fanfic *Godfeels*,
which I quite enjoyed the first chunk of and then kind of lost interest when
they went into a spaceship fleeing from Earth C.
